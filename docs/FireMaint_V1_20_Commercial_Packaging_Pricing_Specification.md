# FireMaint V1 — Commercial Packaging & Pricing Specification

## Bismillah.

This artifact converts FireMaint from software into a sellable SaaS offer.

# Pricing Hypothesis

Indicative Malaysian pricing:

## Starter
RM99–149 / month

For:
- small contractors
- few users
- basic reporting

## Professional
RM249–399 / month

For:
- growing maintenance companies
- multiple technicians
- client portal
- offline workflow

## Business
RM599–999 / month

For:
- larger contractors
- more users/sites
- advanced configuration/support

## Enterprise
Custom

For:
- multi-branch
- higher limits
- custom onboarding/integration

# Setup / Onboarding

Indicative:

RM500–2,000 one-time

Depending on:
- data import
- template configuration
- report branding
- training
- QR setup

# Packaging Dimensions

Possible limits:
- users
- technicians
- clients
- active equipment
- storage
- reports/month
- custom templates
- support tier

Avoid pricing by every minor feature.

# Recommended V1 Packaging

Starter:
- 2 office users
- 3 technicians
- client/site/equipment
- inspections
- reports

Professional:
- more users
- offline
- client portal
- custom templates
- dashboard

Business:
- higher limits
- onboarding assistance
- priority support
- multi-site operations

# Commercial Rules

- monthly or annual billing
- annual discount optional
- onboarding charged separately
- no accounting/payroll bundle in V1
- no custom-code promise in base plans

# Validation Needed

Pricing remains a hypothesis until validated with:
- 5–10 contractor interviews
- willingness-to-pay discussions
- competitor review
- first pilot customers

## Principle

> **Price the operational value: less admin, faster reporting, stronger evidence.**
